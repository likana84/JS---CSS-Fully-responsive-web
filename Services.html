MEDIUM WEIGHT

HEAVY WEIGHT

Any goods weighing above 5000t is considered heavy weight and requires special handling.For that matter, we are obliged to take extra caution. Our charges varies, depending on the weight of the goods and the distance to be covered, and also risks involved, that is to say: handling some goods need extra care not to cause accidents. Finally, roads that lead to the destination must be put into consideration.

For more information...

PERISHABLE GOODS

Some goods go bad very quickly, therefore, the need to quickly deliver them to the destination becomes a priority.In this case, We offer special transport just for perishable goods.This transport is faster and reliable with experienced drivers and tested overtime.All goods are insured just in case there is an accident.
